Our Story

From a small family idea to a trusted supplier for thousands of Australian homes and businesses.

Who We Are

A family business built on freshness

JoyNuts was founded by Jimmy & Family as a small, family-run business in Melbourne. What started as a passion for great food has grown into a pioneering force in the Australian food industry, now serving over 2,500 customers Australia-wide.

We roast our nuts on-site, every day, so what leaves our warehouse is always fresh. Our range has grown to cover herbs & spices, cocoa products, dried vegetables, pulses, seeds & grains, and an abundant variety of fruits & nuts — but the standard we hold ourselves to hasn't changed.

How We Work

Roasting technique, taken seriously

Whether it's oil, dry or flavour roasting, our techniques are widely regarded as some of the best in Australia — and every product is tested for quality before it ships.

Sourcing

We work with suppliers who share our ethos of sustainability, ethical farming and excellence, so every ingredient starts on the right foot.

Roasting & Flavouring

Nuts are oil, dry or flavour roasted fresh on-site every day, using techniques refined over years of specialised roasting.

Quality Testing

Every batch is tested for taste, texture and safety, ensuring it meets our stringent quality standards before it's approved.

Blending & Packaging

Products are blended and packed — including for our wholesale partners — with an eye toward eco-friendly packaging solutions.

Delivery

From our Clyde North base, we deliver retail and wholesale orders to customers right across Australia.
